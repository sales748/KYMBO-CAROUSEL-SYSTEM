# Trading / Stocks / Bolsa — Platform Safety Guidelines

**Apply these whenever the content is about trading, stocks, crypto, investing,
markets, or any regulated financial topic** — on any profile. TikTok (and IG to
a lesser degree) actively suppress finance content that reads as investment
advice or a return promise. Carousels are hit hardest because the platform runs
**OCR on the text in every image**, so on-screen words matter as much as the
caption.

## Why finance content gets 0 views / shadowbanned

TikTok's *Regulated Goods & Financial Services* + *Community Guidelines* policies
moderate content that could cause "financial harm". The triggers:

1. **Specific investment advice** — telling people to buy/sell a named asset, or
   answering "should I buy $X?". A "this stock is a great setup" post = advice.
2. **Promised / projected returns** — "+24%", "+20%", "10x", "setup perfecto",
   "esto va a subir", price targets presented as predictions.
3. **Return comparisons** — pitting a "safe" yield vs an asset ("10% CDT vs +24%").
4. **Unoriginal content** — screenshots carrying another app's watermark
   (TradingView logo, etc.), reposts, low-effort duplicate frames.
5. **Non-original / copyrighted audio** — use the Commercial Music Library or
   original sound, not a random trending song on a finance post.
6. **Spam behavior** — posting many finance videos fast, aggressive off-platform
   CTAs, link-in-bio pushes.

## The rules (do / don't)

**DON'T (these get OCR-flagged on carousels):**
- ❌ "setup perfecto / seguro / garantizado", "esto va a subir", "cómpralo"
- ❌ Return figures as promises/targets: "+24%", "+20% asegurado", "$1.2M"
- ❌ Compare returns ("10% CDT o +24%")
- ❌ Frame any slide as a buy/sell call on a named ticker
- ❌ Leave third-party watermarks (TradingView logo) on chart screenshots
- ❌ Off-platform "link en bio para la señal", paid-signal CTAs

**DO:**
- ✅ Frame as **education / analysis**, never a recommendation:
  "así analizo…", "cómo leer…", "4 señales que estudié…"
- ✅ Prefer **qualitative** over hard numeric promises ("ha tendido a reaccionar"
  instead of "+20%"). If a number is essential, tag it **"(ilustrativo)"** and
  frame it as *context/distance*, never a *target/ganancia*.
- ✅ Put a **visible disclaimer on-slide** (every slide, small): *"Contenido
  educativo · No es asesoría financiera"* — and a fuller one in the caption.
- ✅ Ask **educational engagement** questions ("¿conocías estas señales?",
  "guárdalo para estudiarlo"), not "¿compro o no?".
- ✅ Use **clean, original** chart screenshots — crop/hide the TradingView logo.
- ✅ Use **original audio** or the Commercial Music Library.
- ✅ Keep hashtags educational and moderate (#educacionfinanciera,
  #analisistecnico, #aprenderainvertir) rather than pump tags.

## Word swaps (banned → safer)

| Evita | Usa |
|---|---|
| setup casi perfecto | 4 señales técnicas se alinearon |
| +24% hasta el ATH | cotiza con descuento frente a su máximo (contexto) |
| históricamente sube +20% | históricamente ha tendido a reaccionar al alza |
| ¿10% CDT o +24%? | ¿conocías estas señales? guárdalo para tu análisis |
| esto va a subir / cómpralo | esto es lo que yo observo (análisis, no consejo) |

## If a post is suppressed (0 views for hours)

1. **Delete or set the post to private** (a flagged post can drag the account).
2. **Pause posting ~48–72 h.** Don't re-upload the same file back-to-back.
3. Repost the **de-risked version** (this checklist applied) with **original
   audio**.
4. Keep the account's finance posts consistently educational — repeated flags
   train the account-level filter.
